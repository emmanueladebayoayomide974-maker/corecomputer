Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At CoreComputer, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of data strings that are collected and recorded by CoreComputer and how we utilize them [INDEX].

Log Files

CoreComputer follows a standard technical protocol of using log files. These files automatically log visitors when they navigate websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp parameters, referring/exit pages, and tracking click metrics [INDEX].

Cookies and Consent Tokens

Like any other professional website, CoreComputer uses cookies to store user preference variables and optimize background layout script execution speeds [INDEX]. This data token process improves your browsing environment and helps serve targeted assets through our layout modules [INDEX].

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is an essential third-party analytics and advertising vendor on our site. It uses tracking tokens, known as DART cookies, to serve tailored advertisements to our site visitors based upon their visit history across CoreComputer and other portals on the public internet [INDEX]. Visitors can choose to decline or manage these cookies via our baseline landing selection box controls [INDEX].

Our Advertising Partners

Some of the advertisers integrated across our display columns may utilize tracking identifiers and web beacons. Our baseline partner configuration utilizes Google AdSense. Third-party ad servers use advanced scripts like JavaScript or specialized cookies directly within their ad components to automatically analyze click CTR metrics [INDEX]. CoreComputer has no direct backend command access over these third-party trackers.
